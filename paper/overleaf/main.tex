\title{Connectivity-Governed Execution Authority for Agentic Edge Computing in Intermittently Connected Underwater Networks}

\author{IEEE Communications Magazine~---~draft companion to the CGEA underwater repository%
\thanks{Experimental freeze and campaign status: \texttt{docs/EXPERIMENT\_STATUS.md} in the companion repository.}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Underwater acoustic networks are intermittently connected: authority, telemetry, and task coordination do not share a reliable control channel. We specify \emph{Connectivity-Governed Execution Authority} (CGEA), a non-LLM execution governor that contracts an authority capsule as connectivity and freshness degrade. The scientific design is frozen: paper-risk-bounded capsule grants, locked utility, and a safe-useful-retention metric that does not penalize refusal of mission-beneficial but hard-safety-violating actions. Validation uses Bellhop-generated channels, a Sionna OFDM Monte Carlo PHY, and shared GPU traces replayed by five baselines.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
Underwater acoustic networks, agentic edge computing, execution authority, intermittent connectivity.
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}

Agentic edge systems propose actions under local observations. In underwater acoustic settings, partitions and delay make unsupervised consequential actions a safety problem, not only a throughput problem~\cite{akyildiz2005,stojanovic2007}. CGEA separates a heuristic mission planner from a deterministic governor. The governor cannot be bypassed. Capsules are issued by a gateway and contract with authority age; they are not learned policies.

This draft does \textbf{not} treat diagnostic $n{=}3$ campaigns as paper evidence. Production tables and the four manuscript figures are generated only from the frozen $n{=}10$ grid documented in the companion repository.

\section{CGEA in brief}

A proposal carries action type, energy, risk class, and rationale. The governor returns ALLOW, DENY, or DEFER with a reason code. \textbf{Low-risk} inspection actions are allowed when not otherwise expired. Four hard-safety classes remain \textbf{forbidden} under the paper capsule even when the capsule is fresh: exclusion-zone entry, reserve violation, abandoning a mandatory segment, and changing the high-level objective. \textbf{Reassignment} of a failed vehicle's incomplete mandatory segment is \textbf{conditional}: the target must be failed, the segment mandatory and incomplete, and the capsule recruit limit must not be exceeded. The governor evaluates that condition; it does not blindly defer every conditional action under PARTITIONED, and it does not copy the evaluation oracle at runtime.

Freshness bands (aging 180\,s, stale 400\,s, hard expiry 900\,s, refresh 60\,s) contract allowed sets further. They are not retuned from experimental outcomes. After a forced outage, reconciliation is per vehicle: DIGEST, optional PROVENANCE, RECONCILE, AUTHORITY. A vehicle may leave RECOVERING when \emph{its} digest succeeds. Governance packets follow the existing connectivity-graph shortest path; DATA cannot occupy a small reserved queue tail. That is not a new MAC or routing protocol.

\section{Experiment freeze}

Utility weights are locked as \texttt{utility\_v2\_frozen\_2026-09-28}. The primary retention metric is
\[
\text{safe useful retention}
=
\frac{
\#\{\text{ALLOW}\cap\text{beneficial}\cap\neg\text{hard-safety}\}
}{
\#\{\text{proposed}\cap\text{beneficial}\cap\neg\text{hard-safety}\}
},
\]
frozen as \texttt{safe\_useful\_retention\_v1\_2026-09-28}. Legacy useful retention is retained in logs only.

Channels: three predeclared SSPs at 200\,m; GPU PHY traces; one \texttt{trace\_id} per (environment, seed); baselines B1--B5 including a-priori B5 conservative/nominal/permissive. Outage durations $\{0,150,300,500,750,1000\}$\,s on a 1400\,s mission. Pairwise comparisons share the same trace: primarily B4${}-{}$B5$_{\text{nominal}}$, also B4${}-{}$B3. B5 conservative and permissive are robustness bounds.

Propagation uses Bellhop/AUBellhop~\cite{porter2011}; Sionna~\cite{hoydis2022sionna} is used for OFDM Monte Carlo, not as a radio geometric underwater model.

\section{What this paper will and will not claim}

CGEA claims \emph{risk-bounded execution authority} only insofar as the frozen capsule forbids the four hard-safety classes independently of expiry, and contracts further when stale. It does not claim learned planning~\cite{chen2023agents}. Diagnostic campaigns established that a broad capsule plus capsule-id conflict scoring and all-to-all digest barriers are not paper-valid; those folders remain in \texttt{results/} for provenance and are not manuscript evidence.

Production headline metrics: mission utility; hard-safety violation rate; safe useful retention; semantic conflicts by kind; governance, mission, and total TX bytes; GCO; per-AUV reauthorization fraction, latency, and timeout fraction.
